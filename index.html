Introduction to Smart Farming

Smart agriculture is the use of modern technology, scientific methods and efficient resources in farming. It helps farmers improve productivity, save water and reduce farming costs.

Types of Crops

🌾

Rice

Rice is one of the important food crops grown in many parts of India.

🌱

Ragi

Ragi is a nutritious millet crop suitable for dry farming conditions.

🌽

Maize

Maize is widely used as food, animal feed and for industrial purposes.

🍬

Sugarcane

Sugarcane is an important commercial crop used mainly for producing sugar.

Modern Farming Methods

💧 Drip Irrigation

Delivers water directly to plant roots and helps reduce water wastage.

🌱 Organic Farming

Uses natural materials and reduces the use of synthetic chemicals.

🚜 Precision Farming

Uses data and technology to manage crops efficiently.

Modern Agriculture Technologies

🚁 Drones

Drones can help monitor large agricultural fields and crops.

📡 Sensors

Sensors can monitor soil moisture, temperature and other conditions.

📱 Smart Apps

Mobile applications can provide farmers with useful agricultural information.

Farmer Tips

✔ Use water efficiently.

✔ Choose crops suitable for the soil and climate.

✔ Check soil health regularly.

✔ Use modern farming technology when useful.

✔ Protect crops from pests and diseases.

✔ Keep records of farming activities and expenses.
